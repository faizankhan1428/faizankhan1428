### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a results-driven **AI Engineer** specializing in state-of-the-art machine learning architectures, autonomous agentic workflows, and real-time computer vision systems. My core engineering ethos bridges abstract AI research with robust, scalable production deployments.

My technical specialization focuses on building high-performance deep learning models (YOLOv8 / YOLOv11), integrating Vision-Language Architectures, and optimizing complex neural networks for low-latency execution. I leverage advanced prompt engineering frameworks, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and modern AI development tooling (Cursor, Antigravity, Devin) to construct autonomous intelligence that solves high-impact real-world problems.
